## Evidence discipline

External evidence is recorded separately in [EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE.md](EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE.md).

Each record must distinguish:
- the exact artifact or revision that was reviewed;
- what the external party actually reproduced, challenged, or observed;
- what changed afterward;
- whether the changed revision was independently rerun;
- and the limits of what the record establishes.

Do not collapse review of one revision into validation of a later revision.
